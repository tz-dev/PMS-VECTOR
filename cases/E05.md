# E05 — Premature Enactment

**Paper:** Appendix E.5  
**Format:** Micro-Countercase  
**Adversarial:** No  
**Load-bearing:** No

## Tempting Misclassification

An organization has a well-supported preliminary DIR (Direction) favoring a new procedure. A tempting move is to treat that directional support as sufficient reason to bind the organization publicly to the change immediately.

That move collapses two different questions. DIR concerns the bounded directional relation; enactment timing has its own burden.

## Minimal Countercase

At the relevant pre-binding moment, a low-cost affected-role ROT (Rotation) remains available, and one serious rival comparison can still be performed. The organization nevertheless publicly binds itself to the change before using the cheap materially relevant affected-role test.

The failure is therefore not established as a bad direction. A later ROT may or may not revise the preliminary DIR. What is already established is that public binding prematurely narrows review and correction space while cheap material pressure remains available.

Derived DIST (Distance) may summarize that narrower orientation–enactment separation through source relations such as publicness, fixation, and review space. It is not the mechanism that caused the failure and adds no independent warrant.

## Correct VECTOR Result

The baseline implication requires revision:

\[
DIR_{robust}
\not\Rightarrow
E_{immediate}.
\]

The favorable preliminary DIR may survive. What does not survive is the inference that directional support by itself licenses immediate public binding. The correct diagnosis is **premature enactment relative to available correction space**.

No applied ROT result is needed to reach that timing diagnosis: the fixture itself supplies the cheap material test that remained available and the public binding that occurred before it. The affected-role ROT remains unperformed at the relevant pre-binding moment, while the serious rival comparison remains additional open pressure for any stronger claim about the procedure itself.

## What Does Not Follow

- Premature enactment does not imply that the underlying DIR was false.
- A well-supported or robust DIR does not imply immediate enactment.
- Derived DIST does not independently cause or warrant the timing diagnosis.
- Rejecting premature binding does not imply that delay is universally preferable.

## Repository Boundary

E05 is a paper-linked timing and corrigibility fixture. It does not validate VECTOR, supply the missing ROT or rival result, or create a universal optimal waiting rule. Its function is to preserve the paper's separation between directional support and the timing of enactment when cheap correction remains available.
