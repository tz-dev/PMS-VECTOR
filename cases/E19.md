# E19 — Echo Convergence

**Paper:** Appendix E.19  
**Format:** Micro-Countercase  
**Adversarial:** No  
**Load-bearing:** No

## Tempting Misclassification

Five local analyses all support a similar directional conclusion. It is tempting to treat the repeated DIR (Direction) outputs as multiple independent supports for a common resultant orientation:

\[
d_1,d_2,d_3,d_4,d_5\rightarrow DIR_R.
\]

That move counts outputs before checking whether their material warrant is actually distinct.

## Minimal Countercase

Dependency audit shows:

\[
d_1,\ldots,d_5\leftarrow A,
\]

where \(A\) is the same untested assumption imported into every local analysis.

The five outputs therefore contain nominal multiplicity but one shared unresolved dependency at the relevant point. PMS-VECTOR's convergence provenance is a dependency audit: recurrence, shared genealogy, or repeated direction cannot manufacture independent support.

No additional ROT (Rotation) is needed to reach this fixture's reduction: the shared dependency already defeats the claim of five independent warrants. Relevant ROT remains part of any later robust-convergence burden. A stronger claim may re-enter only after \(A\) is separately pressured.

## Correct VECTOR Result

The convergence claim requires revision:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Five echoes}
\neq
\text{five warrants}.
}
\]

The five similar local outputs remain recorded, but at the current audit state they cannot be counted as five independent warrants for \(A\) or as sufficient support for robust \(DIR_R\). The current result is therefore **Revision Required** for the stronger convergence claim.

## What Does Not Follow

- Exposure of the shared assumption does not by itself show that every local \(d_i\) is false.
- Five similar outputs do not provide five independent warrants for \(A\).
- Apparent recurrence does not establish robust convergence or a warranted resultant \(DIR_R\).
- The case does not establish that a resultant orientation is impossible; it requires separate pressure on \(A\) before the stronger claim can re-enter.

## Repository Boundary

This fixture tests convergence provenance and echo control, not the empirical truth of assumption \(A\). It does not turn source lineage into epistemic ranking and does not infer new dependency edges from co-occurrence. `cases/E19.yaml` records only the dependency relation declared by the paper-linked fixture.
