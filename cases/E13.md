# E13 — Supported Indirect Cost versus Invented Cost

**Paper:** Appendix E.13  
**Format:** Full Minimal Case  
**Adversarial:** No  
**Load-bearing:** No

## Purpose

This case tests whether VECTOR can preserve a real downstream consequence when its evidential bridge is traceable while refusing to invent a positive hidden cost where only unspecified possibility is supplied. The fixture is about consequence-evidence typing, not about maximizing the number of costs represented.

## Case Setup

### Reference, Frame, and Question

The case compares two downstream-cost assertions.

In **Scene A**, a maintenance deferral leaves a documented failure mode active. The supplied technical record identifies the mechanism connecting the deferred repair to later outage risk, the bearer is the service that depends on the component, and the future temporal relation is explicit.

In **Scene B**, the only challenge is that there may be some downstream cost not yet thought of. No bearer, mechanism, temporal relation, or external support is supplied.

The bounded question is therefore: **which downstream assertion can be carried as a positive consequence relation, and which must remain Unknown/Residual?**

### Baseline Claim

The tempting conflation is that both scenes involve unseen downstream possibilities and therefore both can be treated as positive cost claims.

E13 tests that conflation directly.

## Warrant and Vulnerability

Scene A is not directly observed as a future outage event, but the relation is sufficiently traceable for a bounded inference. The record preserves the service as bearer, the future temporal relation, the documented failure mode as mechanism/source, and the technical record as the evidential bridge. Its evidence status is therefore **traceably inferred**.

Scene B lacks precisely those relations. The absence of a specified bearer, mechanism, temporal relation, and support does not establish that no downstream consequence exists. It also does not establish that one does. The correct state is unresolved residue. **Unknown/Residual here is a consequence-evidence status; it neither establishes a positive consequence nor licenses an option-status inference.**

PMS-SEX supplies only a lineage precedent that binding and exposure can leave downstream effects after exit. It does not warrant the maintenance-deferral relation or the E13 evidence classification. PMS-VECTOR owns the explicit evidence-status architecture and the rule that unsupported downstream possibility remains residue rather than becoming a positive cost claim.

## Pressure / Test

No ROT (Rotation) is applied in this fixture. The declared discrimination comes from applying the same consequence-evidence burden to both scenes.

Scene A survives that burden because its bearer, temporal relation, mechanism/source, and evidential bridge are traceable. VECTOR may therefore record a delayed/downstream consequence while preserving uncertainty about magnitude and exact timing.

Scene B does not survive as a positive cost claim. Its unspecified possibility remains `unknown_or_residual`; it is deliberately **not** inserted as a positive consequence item.

## Result

The case returns **Revision Required**.

Scene A is retained as a traceably inferred downstream consequence. Scene B remains Unknown/Residual. The baseline conflation—treating unseen possibility as sufficient for positive cost status in both scenes—is rejected and must be revised.

This result is deliberately limited. E13 does not establish the exact magnitude or timing of Scene A's future cost, and it does not establish that Scene B contains no downstream consequence. It records the difference between supported inference and unsupported residue.

## Architecture Effect

No architecture change is enacted. E13 is a local discriminative fixture for the existing Consequence Topology / Evidence Status architecture.

## What Does Not Follow

- Unseen does not mean zero.
- Unseen does not mean invented.
- Unknown residue cannot function as a universal defeat condition for an otherwise supported DIR (Direction) or option claim.

## Repository Boundary

This case is a paper-linked discriminative fixture. It does not empirically validate VECTOR, quantify downstream risk, or create hidden evidence. Its function is to preserve the paper's distinction between a traceably inferred consequence relation and an unsupported remainder that must stay Unknown/Residual.
