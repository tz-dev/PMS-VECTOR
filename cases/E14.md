# E14 — `OUGHT_account` — Orientation-Mediated Attribution Increase

**Paper:** Appendix E.14  
**Format:** Full Minimal Case  
**Adversarial:** No  
**Load-bearing:** No

## Purpose

This case tests whether a later, sufficiently supported orientation can change practical attribution without rewriting the independent baseline carried by PMS-LOGIC. `OUGHT_account` is used only for the narrower VECTOR relation in which a relevant claim has actually become available to the actor for contest or response.

## Case Setup

### Reference, Frame, and Question

A role already possesses effective capacity over a process and a known consequence relation. Any structural attributability supported by that configuration under PMS-LOGIC is the baseline. VECTOR does not insert an awareness requirement into that prior source relation.

A later review then produces a bounded relation \(R\) identifying an additional, previously unintegrated consequence of the process. Where external support is required, it is supplied outside VECTOR. The relation becomes actor-accessible, is understood well enough to be contested or acted on, and remains praxis-available inside the relevant scene-time window \(t_s\).

The bounded question is whether those conditions support an **orientation-mediated increase in structural attributability** and therefore the VECTOR relation `OUGHT_account(R)`.

### Baseline Claim

Before the later review, only the independently supported PMS-LOGIC baseline is established. Mere later notification does not retroactively alter that baseline and does not by itself establish an additional VECTOR attribution relation.

## Warrant and Vulnerability

The orientation-mediated relation depends on the full bundle declared in the paper:

\[
RelevantOrientation
+
ExternalPremiseSupport
+
ActorUptake
+
EffectiveCapacity
+
ConsequenceRelation
+
Role/TimeConditions.
\]

The bundle is conjunctive at the level of the fixture, but the failure routes remain typed. If Actor Uptake fails, the orientation-mediated uplift is not established while any independently supported PMS-LOGIC baseline remains separate. If effective capacity or the relevant role/time conditions fail, `OUGHT_account(R)` must reduce, retype, or suspend for the bounded scene. If a claim-critical external premise or the consequence relation supporting \(R\) is defeated, the relation must weaken, reduce, or suspend accordingly. Presentation alone cannot rescue any of these failures. The notation does not manufacture external factual support, and the case supplies no universal numerical threshold.

PMS-LOGIC provides the lineage for effective, frame-bound capacity and structural attributability without prescriptive ought. It also preserves the boundary that effective potential can support responsibility without generating obligation. PMS-VECTOR owns the additional orientation-uptake chain, the distinction between `BaselineAttributability_LOGIC` and `DeltaAttributability_orientation`, and the claim type `OUGHT_account`.

## Pressure / Test

### Orientation Uptake

The later review presents \(R\), but presentation alone would be insufficient. In this fixture, however, the stronger conditions also carry: \(R\) is actor-accessible, sufficiently understood, and praxis-available within the relevant window.

The distinction remains:

\[
Presented
\neq
ActorAccessible
\neq
Understood
\neq
PraxisAvailable
\neq
Enacted.
\]

Understanding does not require agreement. Praxis-availability means the relation can be verified, disputed, rejected, refused, or acted upon. The actor's eventual response is therefore left open.

No ROT (Rotation) is applied in this fixture. The discrimination is carried by the uptake and attribution dependency bundle together with the explicit LOGIC-baseline boundary.

## Result

Under the supplied conditions, the paper permits:

\[
\boxed{
\Delta Attributability_{orientation}>0
}
\]

and:

\[
\boxed{
OUGHT_{account}(R).
}
\]

The result means that later praxis cannot reconstruct \(R\) as though the newly available relation had never entered the actor's practical field. It does **not** mean that the actor must choose a particular response.

The record remains bounded to \(R\), the declared role and process relation, the supplied external support, and the relevant scene-time conditions. The general sufficiency threshold remains calibration-sensitive and non-universal.

## Architecture Effect

No architecture change is enacted. E14 instantiates the existing VECTOR `OUGHT_account`, Orientation Uptake, and structural-attributability architecture.

## What Does Not Follow

- Being told about \(R\) does not imply Actor Uptake of \(R\).
- `OUGHT_account(R)` does not imply `OUGHT_choice(X)`.
- The VECTOR orientation-mediated increase does not replace or erase the independent PMS-LOGIC baseline.

## Repository Boundary

This is a paper-linked discriminative fixture, not empirical validation of VECTOR and not an extension of PMS-LOGIC into a prescriptive ought theory. It records the paper's bounded case in which the required orientation-mediated dependencies are supplied while the baseline responsibility relation and external-warrant boundary remain explicit.
