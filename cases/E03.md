# E03 — Incomparability

**Paper:** Appendix E.3  
**Format:** Micro-Countercase  
**Adversarial:** No  
**Load-bearing:** No

## Tempting Misclassification

Two emergency procedures answer different, non-substitutable questions. Procedure X minimizes immediate service interruption; Procedure Y maximizes preservation of audit evidence needed to determine a later cause.

The tempting move is to treat the absence of an overall rank as equality:

\[
X = Y.
\]

That is not the result licensed by the case.

## Minimal Countercase

The declared comparison remains meaningful, but the current situation supplies no warranted priority relation between the two aims, and neither procedure dominates the other on both.

DIR (Direction) therefore does not produce a dominance relation. The correct bounded relation is:

\[
\boxed{X\parallel_{V,C}Y.}
\]

The procedures remain materially different. What is absent is warranted rank, not difference.

## Correct VECTOR Result

**Incomparable.**

The comparison is meaningful, but no warranted dominance relation has been earned under the declared conditions. A later warranted priority relation or genuine cross-aim dominance would constitute a new bounded comparison burden; neither is supplied here.

## What Does Not Follow

- Incomparability does not imply equality.
- Absence of rank does not imply equal quality, consequence, adequacy, or practical significance.
- Incomparability is not Non-Capture: the comparison itself remains meaningful.
